Every planned item is implemented. The branch departs from the plan, as recorded in the ledger at the time, in these places:

1. **Tasks 5 and 7 each ran as two dispatches,** 5a and 5b, 7a and 7b, because the `[Criterion]` and `SystemOneDecisionClient` renames each reach the generator, the analyzers and a dozen docs pages.

4. **The documented-types guard recognises attributes by their usage form,** such as `[Criterion(`, and extension classes by their methods, such as `UseCaching`, because users never write `CriterionAttribute` or the extension class's name.
5. **CORE-58 is the `Failures` constructor only.** Its `IsCached` half is covered by CORE-12, which makes `IsCached` settable at construction.
