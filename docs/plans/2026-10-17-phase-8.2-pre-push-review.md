Notable rulings:
- **The runner:** a worker pool. The first client fault stops the run.
- **Bad responses:** a negative or non-finite cost or token count becomes unknown. A response with a kind mismatch, or that answers nothing, makes the item an InvalidResponse error.

- **Output types:** reshaped to sealed classes with internal constructors, plus `AnswerRecord` factories, before the API freezes in 8.3.
- **InvalidResponse items:** they count toward cost.
- **Noul sweep coverage:** an answer counts when its confidence or its probability clears the threshold. This fixes the `1 − p` binary-rounding gap.
