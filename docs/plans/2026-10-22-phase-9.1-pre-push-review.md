## Plan adherence

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-phase-9.1-recorder.md`, executed through subagent-driven development.
- **Task reviews:** Task 1 had two fix rounds, Task 2 two, Task 3 one, and Task 4 was approved with minors.
- **The final review:** a whole-branch review ran on the most capable model, followed by one fix wave and a scoped re-review.
- **The trim:** the maintainer then asked whether this was overengineered, and the package was trimmed before release. A review of the trimmed branch found one flaky test and two minors, all fixed.

Notable rulings:
- **The queue:** records are written from a bounded background queue, by the maintainer's decision. A full queue drops records, never outcomes or erasures. An erasure completes only after the sink ran it, or it fails.
- **The recorder:** an app-owned `DecisionRecorder` is shared by every pipeline, with one writer per sink. The scope carries the decision id and the erasure key, with no change to `IDecisionClient`.
- **Recording never fails or delays a decision.** The work after the inner call is guarded. Every counter is an observable counter over `Interlocked` totals, so no metric listener runs on the decision path.
- **Dataset format:** v1 gains a `{"json": …}` criterion form, by the maintainer's decision, additively. `Minos.NET.Evaluation` now validates against the published 1.2.0.
- **Line writers:** they are public, by the maintainer's decision, so a database sink can store the documented line and export it.
- **Trimmed before release:** compaction, the forward-compatibility reader, JSONL crash repair, retry attempts in records and the finer metrics are all gone.
  - The shipped sinks are documented as being for development and tests.
  - Retried requests are passed through unrecorded.
  - The source shrank 14 %, and most of what remains is required.
- **Filed:** #254, the caching stage's relative endpoint.
- **Fixed on the way:**
  - the entry-point gate for record structs;
  - a temporary folder left behind when a pack test failed;
  - a race in the AOT smoke app's progress counter;
  - the `RecordingException` constructors, so there is no suppressed diagnostic.
